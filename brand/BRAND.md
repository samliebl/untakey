# untakey brand reference

For anyone (human or Claude session) building anything that carries the untakey look: README, terminal output, site, social image. Use these values; don't eyeball colors.

## Logo

`logo.svg` is the logo: three scooped keycaps in a straight row, receding in perspective to the upper left. The keys are L, `;`/`:` and `'`/`"`. The last one is the hero, in gold, and its legends form a tiny smiley (the `"` ticks are the eyes, the `'` is the smile). `logo-flat.svg` is the flat-top variant, kept as a fallback. `preview.png` shows both on white and on brown. Transparent background, 700×700 viewBox.

The SVG is generated by `make_logo.py` (pure Python, no dependencies; `python3 make_logo.py` writes sheet and SVG files to the current directory). Change geometry or colors there, not by hand-editing paths. Placement: give it clear space of about half a key width on all sides. It works on white, brew brown, cream and blue.

## How the visuals work

- **Flat, no gradients.** Every surface is one solid color. Shine is done with flat pale shapes (glints), never blurs or gradients.
- **Three tones per object.** Top is lightest, front face is mid, right face is darkest. Light comes from the upper left.
- **Outlines are thick-ish, dark ink and rounded** (round joins and caps, slightly rounded corners). They do not appear on the key tops' inner shapes: there is no inner square or dish line, just the scooped edge.
- **Gold is the only hero.** Two beige keys, one gold. In any new asset, gold marks the thing that matters (the free result, the success); beige is everything else. Don't use gold for more than one element at a time.
- **Perspective, not isometric.** Keys are the same size and aligned like a real keyboard row; they shrink with distance because of true perspective. Front edges lie on one line.
- **Scooped tops.** Concave along the row, like real keycaps. Legends are drawn on that curved surface.
- **Legends sit in quadrants.** Treat each key top as four quadrants. All legends live in the left two, padded away from edges: `L` upper left; `:` upper left and `;` lower left; smiley eyes upper left and smile lower left. They are thin strokes, small, a graphic flourish rather than something to read.
- **Personality over polish.** Friendly and a bit cartoonish, never glossy-3D.

## For code (terminal and docs)

Suggestions, not yet applied anywhere:
- Terminal accent: gold `#ffd21f` (256-color fallback `220`) for the "free" status or the hero value; dim or default for everything else. The existing ✓ Free / ✗ Taken output is a natural place.
- Keep the dark background as `#2e2a24` for any site or image with a dark theme; keep text cream (`#e6dfd0`) on it.
- Respect `NO_COLOR` and non-TTY output: no color there.
